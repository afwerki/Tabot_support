About Tabot

Tabot is an Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo community app designed to help users stay connected with their faith and community.

Features may include:

  • Daily verses and spiritual readings
  • Sermons and Mezmur
  • Church discovery and church information
  • Church services and events
  • Community content
  • News and announcements
  • Messaging
  • Quizzes
  • Notifications
  • English and Amharic language support

Need Help?

If you are having trouble with your account or the app, please contact us through the support details provided for Tabot.

Please include a short description of the issue and, where appropriate, a screenshot. Do not send your password.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tabot ("Tabot", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses and protects information when you use the Tabot mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Tabot may collect information that you provide directly or that is necessary to operate the app.

This may include:

  • Name and email address
  • Account and authentication information
  • Profile information
  • Profile photo
  • Country, city, town or approximate location information
  • Church selections and church-related preferences
  • Language preferences
  • Messages and message attachments
  • Community posts, videos and comments
  • Reactions and other interactions with content
  • Quiz responses and participation information
  • Notification preferences and device notification tokens
  • Reports submitted about content or users
  • Technical information necessary to operate and secure the service

2. Location Information

With your permission, Tabot may use location information to help show churches, services or other relevant information based on your area.

You can deny or change location permission through your device settings. Some location-based functionality may not be available when location access is disabled.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide and operate Tabot's features
  • Personalize relevant church and community information
  • Enable messaging and community interactions
  • Send notifications you have chosen to receive
  • Process reports and moderate community content
  • Maintain the safety and security of the service
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of Tabot

4. User-Generated Content

Tabot includes community features where users may submit content, comments, videos, messages or other materials.

Content may be reviewed or moderated where necessary to enforce community standards, respond to reports, protect users and maintain the integrity of the service.

Reported content may be reviewed by authorized administrators and may be restricted or removed where appropriate.

5. Messages

Tabot may process messages and attachments so that they can be delivered to their intended recipients and so that messaging functionality can operate correctly.

6. Sign-In Services

Tabot may offer account authentication through services such as Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In.

When you use one of these services, we receive only the information made available through that authentication service and permitted by you.

7. Notifications

If you enable notifications, Tabot may store a device notification token so that relevant app notifications can be delivered to your device.

You can disable notifications through Tabot's preferences or your device settings.

8. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by trusted service providers where necessary to host, secure, deliver or operate Tabot.

We may also disclose information where required by law or where reasonably necessary to protect users, prevent abuse, investigate security incidents or protect the rights and safety of Tabot and others.

9. Public Information

Information that you intentionally publish through community features may be visible to other Tabot users. This may include your profile information, posts, comments and reactions depending on the feature being used.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

11. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse and maintain service security.

Information associated with deleted accounts may be deleted or retained for a limited period where legally or operationally necessary.

12. Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their Tabot account through the account settings available within the app.

Account deletion removes or deletes personal information associated with the account subject to information that must reasonably be retained for legal, security or fraud-prevention purposes.

13. Children's Privacy

Tabot is not designed as a service specifically directed to children under 13.

Certain educational or faith-related content may be suitable for families or younger audiences, but the app also contains community, social and messaging functionality.

14. Your Choices

Depending on the functionality available to you, you may be able to update profile information, change notification preferences, control device permissions or delete your account from within the app.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Tabot develops or when legal, technical or operational requirements change.

When we make material changes, the updated policy and revision date will be published on this page.
