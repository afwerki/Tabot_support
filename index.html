Quick guidance for the most common questions.

App troubleshooting

  1. Confirm that your device has a stable internet connection.
  2. Install the latest available version of Tabot.
  3. Close the app completely and open it again.
  4. Sign out and sign back in if the issue relates to your account.
  5. If the issue continues, email us with a screenshot and your device model.

Sign-in help

Tabot supports the sign-in methods shown in the app, including email, Google, and Sign in with Apple where available.

  • Use the same sign-in method you originally used to create your account.
  • For email accounts, use “Forgot password” on the sign-in screen.
  • For Apple or Google accounts, confirm you are signed into the correct provider account.

Delete your Tabot account

You can request permanent deletion from inside the app:

  1. Open Tabot and sign in.
  2. Open Profile.
  3. Select Privacy & Safety.
  4. Select Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps.
Account deletion removes or anonymises the account information associated with your profile. If you cannot access the app, contact support using the email address below.

Privacy and safety

Tabot provides in-app tools for reporting content and managing your account. Please use the report option on relevant user-generated content when needed.

For privacy questions or data requests, contact support@tabot.app.
